Read the original scan's physical layout, not its dictionary identities. Distinguish one printed page with TWO TEXT COLUMNS from a spread containing TWO PRINTED PAGES (usually FOUR text columns). Inspect margins, running heads, folios, gutter, blank sides, prefaces and volume transitions. Portrait orientation is not proof. Do not extrapolate printed numbering. A spread with a blank side is still a spread. Use the supplied coordinate proposal only as a fallible hint. For a definite spread give gutter_x in supplied image pixels at the inter-page whitespace, preserving ALL printed text and folios on both sides. For a genuine single page or wholly blank scan use null. Use uncertain whenever the division cannot be established. State the observed evidence and any unresolved uncertainty; use an empty uncertainty string when resolved. Do not invent a layout from column count alone.
