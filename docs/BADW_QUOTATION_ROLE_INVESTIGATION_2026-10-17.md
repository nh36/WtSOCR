# BAdW quotation-role investigation

This is an investigation record for the next parser tranche, not a grammar
change. It starts from certified `3e441902fbc7bfe765d113133cd14e63886fed7d`.
No release files, citation ownership rules, source observations, or PDF geometry
thresholds were changed. The reproducible occurrence packet is generated by
`python3 scripts/build_badw_quotation_investigation_packet.py` and written to
`data/badw_forensic_structure/quotation_role_investigation.jsonl`.

## Current evidence and limits

The certified corpus has 109,359 established owners and 66 unresolved citation
occurrences (16 HTML, 50 PDF). Meaningful pending alphabetic text is 9,740
characters: 2,587 HTML and 7,153 PDF. The principal relevant PDF families are
4,909 characters of lexical prose without a proven role boundary and 815
quotation/gloss candidates. HTML has 2,295 characters in other heading text,
plus 34 quotation/gloss candidates. These are diagnostic families: they overlap
with neither ownership counts nor accepted semantic roles, and must not be
summed as if they were independent passage counts.

The live occurrence census and disposition ledger were read before sampling.
The seven citations with the explicit `form_substitution_with_quoted_definition_role_missing`
reason are included in the packet with exact source span, text hash, local
context, page and line typography. They are distributed across both PDF
volumes. This is a coherent candidate role sequence: italic Tibetan form or
substitution → Roman quoted German gloss/definition → supporting authority.
The form, gloss/definition and authority should be sibling semantic children
under a source-bounded example/assertion envelope; a source citation remains a
separate authority leaf. The quote delimiters alone cannot decide whether the
quoted text is a translation, a definition, or merely terminology.

The three prior positive facsimile reviews in `quotation_positive_review.jsonl`
remain useful positive controls, but are not represented as newly selected
positives. They confirm a recurring italic-form → Roman translation construction
with the source citation outside the translation. The 36-entry
`quotation_holdout.json` and matching `quotation_review.jsonl` were checked
before selecting examples: the holdout reported no new fragments. Its pages
remain negative/recall controls, not a substitute for an independent
positive-bearing sample. The existing positive facsimile for `sgra sgrog` was
inspected directly: it shows independently set Lex items, an italic Tibetan
substitution followed by Roman quoted material, and later separate lexical
examples; visual adjacency cannot justify merging the Lex assertions.

The packet is deliberately a review packet, not proof that a general rule is
ready. A fresh positive-bearing stratified sample across volumes and independent
entries has not yet been frozen and facsimile-reviewed. In particular, the
seven unresolved examples must each be checked against their actual cached
facsimiles before a rule is proposed. The cached layout records establish page,
printed page, line, x-position, run and font/style spans; they do not replace
visual confirmation.

## Ranked candidates

1. **Italic substitution with a quoted definition and authority.** This is the
   most promising candidate because seven live citations share the explicit
   family label and the existing PDF quotation recovery already distinguishes
   form, quoted text and citation in a narrowly bounded example shape. Start
   boundary evidence should be the italic form/substitution run and its
   independent relation to the printed lemma (including the literal
   substitution marker); end evidence should be the balanced quote plus the
   immediately attached source note, with continuous physical lines and no
   intervening semantic component. Keep Tibetan form, quoted German text and
   source authority as distinct children. Estimate: up to seven owner cases;
   residue gain is unknown and must be measured against spans, not the broad
   4,909-character lexical-prose bucket. Negative controls: the 36 prior
   holdout pages with no accepted fragments, adjacent Lex items and examples
   visible on the inspected `sgra sgrog` page, and all cases with intervening
   prose/component or broken quotation closure. Risk: treating any balanced
   quote after italics as translation; expanding through following citations
   or neighboring Lex material. Generality: plausible, but not established
   until an independent positive sample and all seven sources are reviewed.

2. **Nested example quotation and variant-note hierarchy.** The existing four
   reviewed edge cases include one recoverable balanced nested translation, two
   source-specific punctuation constructions, and one case where repeated
   closing quotation marks leave the boundary genuinely ambiguous. The HTML
   `ka ku sta` example has a citation locator, colon and Tibetan variant inside
   the example; these should be nested variant-note/source children, not a
   second example or translation. Start/end evidence comes from the canonical
   example envelope and exact punctuation/DOM or physical line span. Keep the
   example, translation, source note and variant form distinct. Estimated
   ownership gain: at most the four known occurrences. Negative controls are
   the two damaged/ambiguous quote cases and examples where the citation is
   outside the example. Risk: normalizing unusual punctuation or inferring
   translation role from quotes. This supports a small finite, source-specific
   extension only after fresh examples; it does not support a generic quote
   parser.

3. **Source-language proposals and embedded form/gloss claims.** The current
   corpus has scholar-led proposals and source-language assertions, but the
   existing source-assertion module already requires an accepted enclosing
   scope, finite left-boundary production and independent punctuation at the
   right. A future extension should retain the source-language form, language
   identification, gloss, scholarly claim and citation as separate children.
   Candidate start evidence: a recognized language/form marker or accepted
   definition/sense boundary. End evidence: top-level punctuation, closed
   parenthetical, or a separately bounded following xref. No evidence yet
   supports relaxing the finite productions to general Roman prose plus a
   citation. Coverage estimate is unquantified pending a source-backed sample.
   Negative controls: prose continuing after a parenthesis, comparisons/xrefs
   adjacent to citations, and the 21 damaged Lex cases. Risk: the authority
   note incorrectly determines the claim's left edge or absorbs a subsequent
   comparison.

4. **HTML heading grammatical assertions.** The current residue has 2,295
   heading characters, but only a subset is likely stable grammatical
   description. A possible grammar must be bounded by the descriptive DOM
   sibling of the lemma field and a finite recognized label phrase; arbitrary
   heading prose is not evidence of an assertion. Keep lemma, grammatical
   label, functional description and authority separate. Estimate unknown;
   inspect a stratified DOM sample before asserting coverage. Negative controls
   should include free heading prose, alias lists, and coordinated fields whose
   DOM sibling is not a grammatical-description node. Risk: treating layout
   adjacency as grammatical semantics. No broad heading rule is presently
   supported by this investigation.

The 21 Lex delimiter/decoding failures remain explicitly separate. Missing
quotes or parentheses are source evidence, not parser invitations. No closure
or punctuation repair is recommended. Where the local envelope is not
independently complete, the citation remains unresolved.

## Next implementation session

1. Freeze a new positive-bearing sample from independent entries/pages across
   both PDF volumes, using the prior holdout and three prior positives only as
   exclusion/control sets. Include candidate positives and confusable negatives
   before evaluating any implementation.
2. Retrieve and visually inspect every one of the seven explicit substitution
   cases, then a stratified sample of residue candidates. Save exact source
   object/PDF hashes, source spans, run indices/styles, page geometry and
   facsimile references in the packet.
3. Inspect the corresponding HTML raw DOM cases separately; do not transfer PDF
   typography assumptions to HTML.
4. Decide whether the substitution construction has stable independent
   left/right boundaries across the sample. Implement only if positive and
   negative evidence supports the same finite production; keep current
   quotation recovery and ownership guards intact otherwise.
5. Test targeted entries and boundary negatives first. Measure actual role and
   owner gains, then run full replay only after the candidate grammar has
   survived source review.

At present, the source supports a **candidate** recurring substitution
construction, but not yet a defensible new general grammar: the independent
positive-bearing validation sample and direct facsimile review of the seven
live cases are still missing. Other remaining unbounded prose is blocked by
insufficient passage boundaries unless source review establishes a finite
construction. The next session should begin with that bounded evidence task,
not a neutral quotation absorber.
