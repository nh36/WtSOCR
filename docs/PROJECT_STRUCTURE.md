## Authoritative inputs and generated outputs

Human-reviewed input ledgers include exact Latin overrides, Tibetan-script overrides, supersessions, occurrence-identity checks, signature decisions, and alignment/damage decisions under `data/reviewed_*.tsv`. These files record decisions and evidence; generated diagnostics must not silently replace them.

Generated evidence tables live mainly under `data/tibetan_*.tsv` and `release/current/qa/`. They are reproducible views of the reviewed inputs and current corrected corpus. They should be regenerated by the documented scripts, not manually edited.

`release/current/text/` is deployable corrected text. `release/current/qa/` and `release/current/checksums.tsv` verify and explain that snapshot. `docs/STATUS.md` is generated from the checked-in release and ledgers by `scripts/build_status.py`.

BAdW integration follows a two-layer model: the existing release remains print-faithful, while current BAdW editorial readings and post-print variants belong to a separate source layer. Raw BAdW source caches and decoded corpora remain ignored under `work/`; see [`docs/BADW_SOURCE_INTEGRATION.md`](BADW_SOURCE_INTEGRATION.md).

That source distinction is orthogonal to the four logical scholarly layers documented there: source observations, reviewed/inferred annotations, canonical entities/relationships, and derived representations. The small language/form-mention overlay validates against existing frozen review packets; it neither replaces the lexical contract nor promotes structure or citation ownership. Its reviewed Sanskrit records are development evidence, not independent benchmark gold. Bulk validation outputs remain under ignored `work/`.

`scripts/verify_badw_lexical_source.py` binds parsed BAdW database articles and lexical spans to a frozen source snapshot. `scripts/build_lexical_database.py` turns that verified lexical-record JSONL into a local SQLite/FTS database with source spans, snapshot hashes, occurrence-level citation sigla, exact-span BAdW tooltip-expansion authorities, separate print-bibliography candidates, and optional generated-PDF article witnesses and unassigned fragments. A uniquely linked tooltip establishes a first-party siglum expansion, not verification against the printed bibliography; that status remains separately unverified. PDF witnesses remain a distinct searchable source layer, not conflated with database editorial entries. The builder is non-corrective, and BAdW data belongs in an ignored database under `work/`.

`scripts/badw_bibliography.py` builds a separate authority database using `data/bibliography_database.schema.sql`, entirely offline from cached official `/texte`, `/bibliographie` and `/abkuerzungen` pages. Work, publication and abbreviation identities are separate; source occurrences retain Unicode text, DOM locators, hashes and observation times. HTML citation links use exact same-DOM spans and require agreement between tooltip expansion and the official work row; missing/conflicting rows remain unresolved. `scripts/badw_citation_components.py` preserves exact raw offsets while recognizing complete registered sigla, reversible layout breaks and bounded collection/selector grammars supported by official descriptions. Complete components supersede contained prefixes; genuinely competing identities remain candidates. PDF links reject split-small-cap author false positives and separately identify exact author/year publication references. Unparsed spans remain explicit. These are component links, not proof that every part of a citation or its edition has been resolved.

`data/bibliography_metadata_reviews.tsv` supplies occurrence/hash-pinned structured BibTeX fields; unreviewed records remain partial `@misc` exports retaining original descriptions. `data/bibliography_relation_reviews.tsv` records reviewed explicit online edition statements with exact excerpts and locators. Other author/year relationships remain edition/containment candidates. Neither online metadata nor reviewed online relationships establish compatibility with the printed bibliography. A disagreement between online and printed descriptions must remain a source variant, not an implicit correction.

Registered print OCR supplies fascicle/volume bibliography heading candidates. `scripts/badw_print_bibliography.py` additionally extracts lossless page/text candidates from visually reviewed ranges in `data/bibliography_print_ranges.tsv`, verifying both PDF and OCR hashes. All text, including inherited-author and unclassified spans, is preserved with exact offsets. These are not authority records: OCR, boundaries, continuation across pages, and inherited authors need review before import. The initial range covers the consolidated publication bibliography; remaining work/siglum lists and fascicle supplements still require bounded ingestion and review.

`data/bibliography_print_reviews.tsv` gates item-level import of visually reviewed publication-identity crosswalks and distinct print-only publication authorities. PDF/OCR hashes, exact candidate spans and (for crosswalks) online occurrence hashes are rechecked; printed transcriptions remain separate from online descriptions in `print_occurrence`. Print-only authorities have no fabricated online occurrence and are linked only by exact citation reviews, not global author/year aliases. Reviewed items include Tsepak Rigzin 1986 from the fascicle 22 supplement. This does not certify citation editions or locators. Cross-page/inherited-author import needs an expanded reviewed-span contract, not automatic propagation.

`data/bibliography_publication_reviews.tsv` and `scripts/badw_bibliography_variants.py` preserve hash/excerpt-pinned relationships between distinct publication authorities. Candidate same-work relationships do not merge publications or accept citation links. Tsepak Rigzin 1986 remains distinct from the online 2003 publication, whose description explicitly identifies a reprint of the second revised edition of 1993, not of 1986.

`scripts/query_badw_bibliography.py` offers read-only authority search, original online/print occurrences, relations, citation components and reverse links. Accepted identities and candidate targets are distinct; reverse links exclude candidates by default. No query updates the lexical database or source readings.

`data/bibliography_alias_reviews.tsv` supplies exact, layer-bounded work-identity crosswalks (including printed `HMrg`, `ṅCh`, `Thk`, `Migto`, `Kunk` and `MilGB`). `scripts/badw_bibliography_aliases.py` validates occurrence/hash and registered-print provenance before use with `--alias-reviews` and `--print-registry`. Raw spelling is retained; a reviewed alias does not verify editions or locators. OTM item tooltips require exact collection, edition and selector agreement; spaced MTH selectors require official grammar evidence, not generic whitespace deletion.

`data/bibliography_citation_reviews.tsv` supplies exact citation-level identity reviews via `--citation-reviews`. Original citation hashes, source spans, official occurrence hashes and actual cached/registered evidence objects are checked; stale or orphan reviews fail closed. Spans cannot bisect unknown-glyph markers. Unresolved publication/edition dispositions create no link. These reviews neither alter decoded glyphs nor certify edition or locator accuracy.

`scripts/benchmark_badw_bibliography.py` creates deterministic blind HTML/PDF review packets plus a pinned residual challenge set, then scores reviewed identity spans separately from edition and locator checks. Packets omit resolver predictions and remain under ignored `work/`. Sampling and link coverage are not accuracy measurements; unreviewed packets cannot be counted as correct. Evidence files are hash-verified, and scores are stratified by source layer and membership. Review mode is declared, not proof of reviewer independence; prediction-exposed corrections must not be presented as blind validation. Same-agent source-first review is not an independent human audit. Edition and locator accuracy remain unmeasured. Kramer 2005 has an accepted external publication identity, not a verified locator. Hahn's Lehrbuch work identity is supported by the local 1996 book, while the literal 1992 citation edition remains unresolved. Four exact Maurer 2000 citations now resolve to the 2001 publication through author confirmation reported by the user, retaining literal wording, explicit reviewed year corrections and unverified locators. Online/print year discrepancies must not be silently reconciled.

`data/bibliography_citation_claim_reviews.tsv` adds separate exact citation claims through `--citation-claims`. Cached evidence bytes and actual PDF page bounds, literal citation hashes, locators and uniquely reviewed publication witnesses are checked. Hahn's printed p42 is corroborated in the 1996 witness; this does not certify the cited 1992 edition. A work-identity claim neither accepts that publication as the cited edition nor changes candidate publication edges. An explicit author-confirmed citation-year correction can accept an exact reviewed publication candidate, retaining the literal year and discrepancy relationship without verifying the locator or printing. Its minimal provenance records the user's report date, not an invented email date or public evidence hash; private correspondence is not reproduced. This ledger supplements, rather than replaces, existing print-only authorities and publication relations. Private books remain in ignored storage, never tracked fixtures.

`data/badw_backend_snapshot.json` pins the current verified-source backend and blind review packet by hash; it is not a claim that parsing is correct. `scripts/benchmark_badw_structure.py` scores exhaustive whole-entry annotations using half-open, zero-based Unicode code-point spans in the packet's exact review text (not OCR token indices). It validates sense/example nesting, Tibetan/translation ownership, corrections within Tibetan and citation relationships, and reports exact nodes, nesting, edges and whole-entry agreement separately by source group and holdout split. Reviewers must annotate the complete entry, including missed components, without copying predictions. `reviewed_complete` requires reviewer attribution and declared review mode; pending packets contribute no accuracy. The pinned 120 packets (90 PDF, 30 HTML; 24 holdout) still require actual independent review. No parser rule should be widened from coverage alone or the holdout used to tune it.

`scripts/project_badw_structural_candidates.py` adapts existing PDF nested candidates and HTML lexical records into that benchmark's exact-span contract, offline. It checks source identities, hashes and literal Unicode spans, retains original claims, and projects development packets only. Unnumbered source divisions are not automatically senses; unassigned or shared citations receive no invented ownership. Its complete unassigned-citation inventory and volume-balanced challenge remain pending source review, not accepted links. Original apparatus such as `(r.ka)` is preserved without applying the proposed reading. Adapter output is not a read-only entry-page projection or a verified database import.

The structural benchmark also accepts attributed `reviewed_partial` annotations with explicit unresolved claims: their graphs are validated but excluded from accuracy. Definition citations and evidence-backed shared citation support are distinct relationships; a shared edge requires a common source scope and cannot relax ordinary single-owner containment. PDF packet scores cover the supplied body text, not omitted headings. Exact-span disagreement must be investigated separately as boundary convention, omitted structure or wrong ownership; neither whitespace normalization nor a coverage percentage establishes semantic correctness. Reviews, predictions, diagnostics and copied source text remain under ignored `work/`.

The eight prediction-exposed development reviews currently score 71/121 exact nodes. Their 50 nonmatches comprise 26 component-envelope/model conventions, eight terminal-whitespace differences and 16 language-annotation gaps; no omitted literal material was identified in those reviewed nodes. This is not a finding that all nesting or citation relationships are correct: three exact-component parent disagreements and three exact-endpoint relation disagreements remain for contract/ownership review. A wider development replay exposed and repaired tagged-language projection losses at trimmed HTML Lex. boundaries, using exact clause intersections with their original DOM locators. These diagnoses do not alter gold, scoring or the held-out split, and do not open the semantic extraction, citation-ownership or production gate.

Example (all generated source material stays ignored):

```bash
python3 scripts/badw_bibliography.py --cache work/badw_source_cache \
  --output work/bibliography_snapshot --staging work/staging.sqlite \
  --print-registry data/source_pdfs.tsv \
  --metadata-reviews data/bibliography_metadata_reviews.tsv \
  --relation-reviews data/bibliography_relation_reviews.tsv
python3 scripts/badw_print_bibliography.py \
  --output work/print_bibliography_snapshot
python3 scripts/query_badw_bibliography.py \
  --database work/bibliography_snapshot/bibliography.sqlite search Aris
```

The three official pages must already be cached. Builds refuse an existing output directory, retain the staging/schema hashes, and leave the lexical database and release unchanged. Optional print import requires all three of `--print-registry`, `--print-candidates` and `--print-reviews`; no candidate is imported without its own pinned review. Optional `--publication-reviews` retains reviewed/candidate publication relationships separately. Next steps are independent component-link benchmarking, source-specific unresolved sigla, review of printed supplements and cross-page boundaries, expansion of reviewed metadata/BibTeX, and edition-specific citation resolution; a spelling match alone does not complete those tasks.

For generated PDFs, `scripts/extract_badw_pdf_entries.py` identifies page-local entry starts and `scripts/stitch_badw_pdf_entries.py` joins only verified consecutive-page continuations into source-faithful article witnesses. `scripts/parse_badw_pdf_articles.py` replays those witnesses against cached positioned pages and derives visual lines, typography-supported numbered divisions, quotation/citation/reference candidates, and same-division adjacent quotation–citation pairs. `scripts/benchmark_badw_pdf_structure.py` selects a reproducible stratified review sample and scores those candidates against source-hashed, manually located spans kept under ignored `work/`. These pairs are typographic candidates, not resolved Belegstellen. The parser does not claim a complete definition/Belegstelle parse or silently bridge missing pages. Exact source spans and explicit unknown glyphs remain available; bulk outputs stay under ignored `work/`.

Canonical PDF refresh and review migration use `scripts/refresh_badw_canonical_pages.py` and `scripts/migrate_badw_pdf_reviews.py`: new immutable page trees, source-checked crosswalks, and fail-closed review proposals. Exact malformed-quotation boundaries live in `data/reviewed_badw_pdf_quote_boundaries.tsv`; bulk regenerated corpora and staging databases remain under ignored `work/`.

## Immutable release snapshots

Release tags, input locks, and GitHub release assets describe distinct immutable checkpoints:

| Snapshot | Tag and GitHub release | Lock | Archive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Initial integrated stable checkpoint | `wtsocr-stable-2026-08-06` | `release/inputs/wtsocr-stable-2026-08-06.lock.json` | SHA-256 `c6f74598ef22c738de638608cd3528dbdf65e8f347b0eca831ef28c6bd12a458` |
| Post-alignment-review snapshot | `wtsocr-alignment-review-2026-08-06` | `release/inputs/wtsocr-alignment-review-2026-08-06.lock.json` | SHA-256 `7d4860dbaafae6766065164a897274a5ace5a576a185e0b02226069532db9f31` |

A tag identifies the repository snapshot; its lock identifies the exact production-input payload; the content-addressed release asset supplies those locked files. Assets are immutable and may be retained in more than one release for download continuity, but the lock URL names the release identity authoritative for that snapshot.

See `docs/current_release_workflow.md` and `docs/RELEASE_REPRODUCIBILITY_2026-08-06.md` for the clean-checkout procedure and provenance limitations.

## Historical material

Dated reports under `docs/` preserve audit history, old counts, and decisions as they were understood at the time. They are not silently refreshed. Counts and recommendations may have been superseded; use `docs/STATUS.md` for current work and this file for navigation.

Historical branches are not operational entry points. After normal merge, their ancestry remains reachable through `main` and immutable tags even when redundant branch refs are deleted.
